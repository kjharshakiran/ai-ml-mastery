🧭 The one idea that ties everything together

Almost all of modern AI is the same trick repeated: turn things you care about — words, pixels, sounds, customers — into lists of numbers called vectors, then learn a function that moves those vectors around until useful patterns pop out. Linear algebra moves vectors. Calculus tunes the function. Probability handles the uncertainty. Everything from linear regression to ChatGPT is a richer and richer version of this one idea. Keep it in mind and the whole course clicks into place.
